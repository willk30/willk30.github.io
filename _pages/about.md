I'm Will. I teach and do research in the [Operations Research Department](https://nps.edu/web/or) at the Naval Postgraduate School in Monterey. I finished my Ph.D. at NC State in 2026, after about fifteen years as an Army engineer and operations research analyst.

Most days I'm either working with students — computational methods, data work, getting analyses to actually run at scale — or writing optimization models for load-planning and packing problems. On the side I keep [field notes on AI tools](https://willk30.substack.com) for people who use them to get work done.

If you want papers, talks, or the longer research write-up, those live on their own pages. Email is the best way to reach me: [william.kirschenman@nps.edu](mailto:william.kirschenman@nps.edu).
