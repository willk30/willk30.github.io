This project focuses on developing advanced spatial optimization techniques for complex resource allocation problems, particularly in military and logistics contexts.

## Research Overview

The core of this research involves extending traditional 2-D bin packing algorithms to handle multiple levels of prioritization. This work addresses real-world challenges where items have complex priority hierarchies that must be considered during spatial allocation.

### Key Contributions

- **Multi-level Priority Framework**: Development of a novel prioritization system that can handle nested priority structures
- **Spatial Optimization Algorithms**: Creation of efficient algorithms for optimal placement considering both geometric and priority constraints  
- **Military Applications**: Application to combat load planning and resource allocation scenarios
- **Performance Analysis**: Comprehensive evaluation of algorithmic performance across various problem instances

### Technical Approach

The research combines operations research methodologies with computational geometry to solve complex spatial allocation problems. The framework handles:

1. **Priority Hierarchies**: Items can have multiple levels of priority (high, medium, low) with sub-priorities within each level
2. **Geometric Constraints**: Standard bin packing constraints plus custom shape requirements
3. **Operational Constraints**: Real-world limitations such as weight distribution and accessibility

### Applications

This work has direct applications in:
- Military logistics and combat load planning
- Container shipping optimization
- Warehouse layout optimization
- Manufacturing resource allocation

The research demonstrates significant improvements in allocation efficiency while maintaining operational feasibility.
