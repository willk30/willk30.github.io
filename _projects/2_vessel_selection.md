This project develops automated systems for optimal vessel selection and combat load planning in military logistics operations.

## Project Overview

The research addresses the complex challenge of simultaneously selecting appropriate vessels and planning their load configurations for military operations. This involves multi-objective optimization considering vessel capabilities, cargo requirements, and operational constraints.

### Research Objectives

- **Automated Decision Making**: Develop algorithms that can automatically select optimal vessel configurations
- **Load Planning Optimization**: Create efficient load planning systems that maximize utilization while meeting operational requirements
- **Integration Framework**: Design systems that integrate vessel selection with load planning for holistic optimization

### Technical Components

#### Vessel Selection Algorithm
The system evaluates available vessels based on:
- Cargo capacity and volume constraints
- Operational capabilities and limitations
- Cost considerations and availability
- Mission-specific requirements

#### Load Planning System
The load planning component optimizes:
- Cargo placement for stability and accessibility
- Weight distribution across vessel compartments
- Loading sequence optimization
- Emergency access and unloading procedures

### Implementation Results

The framework has been tested on realistic military logistics scenarios, showing:
- **Efficiency Gains**: 15-30% improvement in vessel utilization
- **Time Savings**: Automated planning reduces manual planning time by 70%
- **Operational Feasibility**: All generated plans meet operational safety and accessibility requirements
