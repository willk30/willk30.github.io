This presentation discusses our work on extending the 2-D bin packing problem to handle multiple levels of prioritization, with applications to military logistics and spatial optimization problems. The research addresses real-world complexity in cargo loading and spatial allocation scenarios.

Co-authors: Heese, H.S., Kay, M., King, R., McConnell, B.
