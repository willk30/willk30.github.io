This presentation discusses our approach to automating combat load planning for military logistics using prioritized 2-D packing algorithms. The work demonstrates significant improvements in planning efficiency and solution quality over manual methods.

Co-authors: Heese, H.S., Kay, M., King, R., McConnell, B.
