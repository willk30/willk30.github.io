01
Wave one · single bin

PRIORITIZED
PACKING

Embed a prioritization matrix into 2-D orthogonal packing. High-priority items draw toward the access point; related items stay clustered. When the MIP will not scale, a sliding-window matheuristic solves overlapping priority-ordered subproblems.

Wave two · one vessel

LOAD
BALANCE

The deck is fixed; the decision is internal arrangement under hard center-of-gravity feasibility, obstacles, and separation. Matheuristic pipelines with in-stride balancing and selective repair finish in minutes — fast enough for time-critical planning.
